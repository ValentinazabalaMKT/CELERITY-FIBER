The first draft used a specific, technical angle (dual-path fiber redundancy / storm season). **Juan José Flores (jefe) reviewed it and redirected the approach:**

> "Ese tema está bueno, pero precisamente como para un artículo de blog... para una campaña de mail no. **Second revision note (2026-08-13):** Juan José updated both CTA URLs: CTA #1 now points to `lets-connect/` (Celerity's contact/assessment form — the one used for Business Journal ads specifically), and CTA #2 now points to the homepage (introduces all three Celerity services, not a blog post).

## Why this reads as editorial, not an ad

- Introduces the company and its value (fiber + managed Wi-Fi + local white-glove service) without a hard sales pitch.
- Speaks equally to Greenfield ("breaking ground on a new development") and Brownfield ("upgrading an existing property") in one sentence.
- No unverifiable claims ("never goes down," "100% reliable," "best in Florida") — consistent with `docs/brand/COPY-GUIDELINES.md` Don'ts.
- General enough for a cold, unsubscribed audience's first touch — technical/seasonal deep-dives are saved for later, warmer touches (see Mailchimp idea bank).
- CTA offers a conversation ("Talk to a Specialist") plus a low-friction "learn more" option, not a hard sell.
