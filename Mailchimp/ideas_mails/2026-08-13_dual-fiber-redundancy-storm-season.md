**Where this belongs instead:** Mailchimp campaigns to **audiences we've already contacted / that are subscribed** — people who already have context on who Celerity Fiber is. This topic is specific and technical enough to work well there (or as a blog/SEO article), but is too narrow for a cold, first-touch Business Journal Announcement to a new, unsubscribed audience.

**Suggested use:** A follow-up nurture email (2nd or 3rd touch) to a list that already received the general "Meet Celerity Fiber" intro, or a standalone blog/SEO piece.

## Topic

**"Why do commercial and multifamily buildings need dual-path fiber redundancy — especially before storm season?"**
Opportunity Score: 80.5/100 (full scoring and 14-question research in `../../Business Journal/Creative One/2026-08-13/04_topic_research.md`, §2–3).

Angle: *"Your building's internet is only as reliable as its weakest single path — and this is Florida."* Educates on dual-path redundancy for both Greenfield (build it in from day one) and Brownfield (assess exposure before storm season), then invites a property assessment / developer briefing conversation.

## Ready-to-use English copy

**Subject Line:** Is Your South Florida Property's Internet Storm-Ready?
**Pre-header:** One fiber path is a risk. Dual redundancy keeps you online.
**Headline:** Storm Season Tests Your Property's Internet. Is It Ready?
**Subhead:** Dual-path fiber redundancy keeps Greenfield and Brownfield properties connected when one path fails.

**Body:**
> A single fiber connection is a single point of failure — and in South Florida, storm season tests that risk every year. Dual-path redundancy gives your property two independent fiber routes, so if one is disrupted, traffic fails over automatically and residents, tenants and building systems stay connected. Whether you're breaking ground on a new development or assessing an existing property, Celerity Fiber builds 100% fiber infrastructure with dual-path redundancy into every property we connect.

**Supporting copy:** Celerity Fiber is a Florida-based fiber provider delivering tailored, white-glove connectivity for developers, property managers and communities across South Florida.

**CTA #1:** Talk to a Specialist → https://www.celerityfiber.com/
**CTA #2:** See How It Works → https://www.celerityfiber.com/future-proofing-mdus-why-dual-fiber-redundancy-is-no-longer-optional
**CTA Button Color:** #582C83
